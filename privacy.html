Privacy policy

A clear explanation of the desktop app, the Google account access it uses, and the choices available to its operator.

01. About this application

Rhen YouTube Automation is a private Windows desktop application operated by the owner of the Rhen YouTube channel. It is used for the operator’s own channel and is not offered as a public account-management service. This website provides application information and this policy; it does not handle Google login, accept OAuth tokens, or manage channel content.

The current application supports Google OAuth authorization, exact-channel verification, saved-token renewal, and local upload-package preparation. Uploading videos, changing video metadata, applying thumbnails, and scheduling releases are planned features that remain disabled. They require further implementation, appropriate permissions, and the operator’s specific approval before use.

02. Data and permissions

The desktop application uses Google OAuth, so the operator authorizes access on Google’s official consent screen without giving the app a Google account password. Access is bound to the configured Rhen channel.

  • Channel information: the authorized channel’s ID, title, uploads-playlist identifier, and relevant account or channel information returned by the YouTube Data API.
  • Video information: when requested for the disclosed workflow, video IDs, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy and audience settings, thumbnail information, and upload or processing status. Access can include private channel information permitted by the authorization.
  • Locally selected assets: approved video and thumbnail files, proposed metadata, file fingerprints, and local job or approval records.
  • Authorization credentials: the application’s OAuth client credentials, a refresh token, short-lived access tokens, and granted-permission information.

The current authorization requests youtube.readonly for channel verification and related reads, and youtube.upload for the intended upload workflow. Upload execution is still disabled. Future scheduling or post-upload metadata changes require an additional appropriate permission, such as youtube.force-ssl, requested through a further consent step when needed. The app does not request Gmail, contacts, Drive, or payment access for this workflow.

03. How data is used

Account data is used to confirm that the operator selected the correct Rhen channel, maintain the authorized connection, prepare and validate local upload packages, and check job or duplicate-upload records. Future approved upload operations would send the selected media and metadata to YouTube, then read back ownership, visibility, and processing results.

Private is the intended upload default. Scheduling, public release, and changes to existing videos require distinct approval. The app does not use Google account data for advertising, audience profiling, data brokerage, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models.

04. Local storage and security

The OAuth client secret and refresh token are stored in dedicated Rhen entries in Windows Credential Manager on the operator’s computer. Access tokens are held in process memory. The app is designed not to print tokens or secrets, put them in command arguments, or write them into project files, upload journals, website files, or public repositories.

Nonsecret operational records—such as channel verification results, granted-scope names, media hashes, approval records, and job status—are stored locally. Account data is exchanged with Google over HTTPS. Windows account and device security remain important: another process with access to the same Windows user account may be able to access stored credentials. There is no claim that a local credential store provides absolute protection.

05. Sharing and service providers

The app sends authorization requests to Google and permitted channel-management requests to the YouTube Data API. Approved videos and thumbnail assets would be sent to YouTube only when their upload or application is authorized. Google’s handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

The operator may direct Codex to review nonsecret connection or job reports to help carry out the disclosed workflow. Those selected reports may therefore be processed by OpenAI under its applicable service terms and privacy policy. OAuth secrets and tokens are kept out of those reports. Google account data is not provided for general-purpose model training or sold to third parties.

This app has no shared account-data backend and does not provide access to advertisers or data brokers. Any additional data transfer must be necessary for the disclosed features and consented to by the operator, or required for security or by law.

06. Google API Services Limited Use

Rhen YouTube Automation’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google API data is used only to provide or improve the disclosed channel-management features. It is not sold, used to serve personalized advertising, or used for unrelated profiling. Human review is limited to the operator’s expressly directed workflow and any review necessary for security or legal obligations. Any permitted service-provider transfer remains limited to these purposes.

07. Retention

The refresh token is retained locally while the operator wants an active connection and the grant remains valid. Google can expire or revoke it; it is not guaranteed to remain valid indefinitely. Short-lived access tokens are not intentionally persisted and expire according to Google’s token lifetime.

The operator handles retention manually. Cached YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 calendar days as applicable under YouTube’s policies; it is not treated as a permanent historical archive. Local source media and operator-authored metadata are distinct from cached API data and remain under the operator’s control. Automatic background data-cleanup and deletion services are not currently enabled.

08. Revoke access or delete data

  1. Stop future Google access: visit Google Account connections, find the Rhen application, and remove its access. Revocation prevents further authorized access; it does not automatically erase every local file.
  2. Remove local credentials: in Windows Credential Manager, remove the dedicated Rhen YouTube client and grant entries. This is a manual operator action; the application does not currently provide an automatic disconnect or deletion button.
  3. Delete local account data: the operator can request or carry out removal of Rhen’s cached API records, connection reports, and related job data. Send a request to sketchbusinessacc@gmail.com. Requests are handled manually, as soon as possible and within seven calendar days. Data associated with revoked access is also removed in accordance with YouTube’s applicable retention rules.

Deleting local app data or revoking Google access does not delete videos, metadata, or other information already stored by YouTube. Manage or delete that content through YouTube’s own controls. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or client secrets by email.

09. This website

This is a static information website prepared for GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, Google sign-in, embedded YouTube players, forms, or browser storage. The logo and styling are served with the site rather than loaded from third-party font or image services.

When hosted publicly, GitHub may process ordinary web-request information, such as an IP address, for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending an email uses the destination service’s own privacy practices. Email correspondence may be retained as needed to answer the request.

10. Contact and policy changes

The responsible operator is the owner of the Rhen YouTube channel. For questions about this policy, account access, or data deletion, contact sketchbusinessacc@gmail.com.

This policy will be updated when the application’s data handling or capabilities change. Any materially different Google data access will be disclosed before use, with additional consent where required. This independent application is not a Google or YouTube product, and this website does not claim Google verification or endorsement.
